    The evidence behind the experience

    History, model, and game are three different layers.

    01

    Historical record

    Passenger fields and outcomes come from the local Kaggle Titanic CSV. Historical photographs and chronology provide context.

    02

    Model interpretation

    Logistic Regression estimates probability from class, sex, age, family, fare, embarkation, title, and deck features.

    03

    Playable interpretation

    Routes, timing, seat capacity, and scores are designed mechanics—not a complete reconstruction of the disaster.
