One outcome is not the pattern. One pattern is not a cause.

The experience now opens into the full passenger record. Analysis is available here whether or not you complete the game.

Begin with sex and class—then complicate the story.

Rates include numerators, denominators, and 95% Wilson intervals. Small cohorts are marked rather than given equal visual authority.

Sex and class intersect

Separate “women” and “first class” bars hide the scale of variation within each class. The strongest first view is their interaction—not either variable alone.

Every mark is a passenger

Age and fare are continuous, unevenly distributed, and incomplete. Unknown age remains a separate lane instead of being replaced with the median.

Travel groups are nonlinear

Small family and ticket groups often show higher recorded survival than solo passengers, while some large groups fared poorly. “Family was better” is too simple.

An apparent port effect changes

Cherbourg has a higher raw survival rate. Stratifying by class shows how passenger composition contributes to that relationship.

Missingness is evidence about the dataset

Cabin is absent for most records. “Unknown deck” cannot be placed on an authoritative ship map, and missing records may reflect class and documentation.

Prediction is another lens—not an explanation of the night.

Logistic Regression and Random Forest are evaluated with stratified cross-validation. Thresholds change which errors the model makes.

What the records cannot prove

The dataset does not record every corridor, crew order, language barrier, delay, family negotiation, lifeboat encounter, or individual decision. Observed disparities describe the surviving record; they do not establish simple causal rules.

  • Game decisions are hypothetical.
  • Model estimates are associative.
  • Historical outcomes are fixed records.
  • Missing fields are not invented.

Integrated data and contextual sources are not the same thing.

Only the local Kaggle passenger table is integrated. Archival sources remain clearly labelled as contextual or candidates until a documented, licensed join exists.
